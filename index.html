🔧 Integration

Live on Your Site
in 3 Simple Steps.

No developer. No hassle. Copy, paste, done.

1

Create Your AI Bot

Sign up, enter your website URL, and click 'Build AI Bot'. Our engine reads your entire site and trains a custom AI in under 60 seconds.

2

Customize & Get Your Code

Go to your dashboard. Set your brand colors, bot name, and welcome message. Then copy the single-line embed code generated for you.

3

Paste & Go Live ✅

Paste the code into your website's <head> or footer. The AI bot appears instantly. Works on WordPress, HTML, and any platform.

Paste this single line inside your <head> or just before </body>:

<script src="https://botbro.editians.com/widget.js" data-bot-id="YOUR_BOT_ID"> </script>

Install "Insert Headers and Footers" plugin from WordPress. Go to Settings → Insert Headers and Footers → paste in Footer Scripts.

<script src="https://botbro.editians.com/widget.js" data-bot-id="YOUR_BOT_ID"> </script>

In Shopify Admin: Online Store → Themes → Edit Code → open theme.liquid → paste just before </body>.

<script src="https://botbro.editians.com/widget.js" data-bot-id="YOUR_BOT_ID"> </script>
